# Credits

All art and sound in this mod was generated for it (nothing taken from Red Dead Redemption 2 or any other game):

- Outlaw, spooked horse, dynamite, cactus, TNT crate and revolver: concept images and 3D models generated with fal (nano-banana-2 and Trellis 2), converted to Quake models by the kit's glb2mdl.py.
- Wanted poster, cash stack and Dead Eye marker: images generated with fal, cut out and converted to Quake sprites.
- Revolver shot, outlaw yell and death, horse neigh, cash pickup, Dead Eye heartbeat, guitar twang and the western theme: generated with fal sound and music models.
- Base game data: LibreQuake (BSD licensed art, GPL progs). Game code: id Software QuakeC 1.06 (GPL).
